B. CLASH — TWO DISTINCT MODES

Clash has two separate modes:
1. DUEL CLASH: a user challenges another user, or one community challenges another community. The invited opponent must accept before the accepted contest proceeds. Participants submit entries and eligible users vote.
2. SOLO CHARACTER CLASH: one user creates an anime-character matchup (for example, Goku versus Saitama). No second user needs to accept. The audience votes for the character they believe is more powerful.

Do not treat these modes as interchangeable.
